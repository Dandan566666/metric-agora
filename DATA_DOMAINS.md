# Data Map and Research Directions

[Home](README.md) · [Design](DESIGN.md) · [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md) · [Participation](CONTRIBUTING.md)

Version 0.1 · 16 October 2026. This is a proposed map of sources and questions, not a collected database. No sources are integrated or continuously monitored by this seed.

## Connect what the world publishes separately

Picture the world's public evidence spread across millions of pages: a local census table, a central bank bulletin, a household survey, a port report, a satellite observation, a research appendix. Each can hold a piece of a larger picture. Much of their value remains out of reach when the connections between them are difficult to find.

We invite people everywhere to draw those connections carefully and openly. Follow a claim from one institution to another. Discover whether two reports truly agree or merely repeat the same source. Find the gap that changes a comparison. Add the local knowledge that a global table cannot supply. This map is a starting point for that collective exploration; every entry is a possible place to begin.

## A map of the world through its evidence

The ambition is broad: make data from any institution or community that lawfully publishes usable evidence discoverable and comparable, wherever it can help answer a question. Official status, commercial scale, and independence are attributes to investigate, not substitutes for evidence quality.

The map should connect three layers: **who publishes the evidence; what is measured; and how observations, transformations, claims, and findings depend on one another.** A user should be able to begin with a new release, explore related measurements, and follow a claim back through its sources.

Comprehensive coverage is a direction of travel, not a promise that every institution or dataset can be accessed. Mark unavailable, restricted, undocumented, discontinued, or unsuitable sources as such. Public visibility does not automatically permit copying, redistribution, or unrestricted automated collection. A reference in this map implies neither affiliation nor endorsement.

## Cross-domain investigation paths

- Births, child survival, migration and age structure → later school cohorts and education capacity.
- Employment, hours, wages and participation → household income, spending, saving and borrowing.
- Production, input use, electricity and freight → inventories, trade, prices and reported output.
- Dwelling construction, household formation, migration and utility connections → occupancy and housing need.
- Crop area, weather, water and yields → food availability, trade, prices and nutrition.
- Public budgets, disbursement, procurement and facilities → delivered services and access.
- Credit, asset prices, debt service and arrears → household and business financial conditions.
- Pollution, weather, work exposure and healthcare access → health outcomes, with careful confounding analysis.

Arrows describe avenues for investigation. They do not establish causality or a fixed numerical conversion. Relationships can change, data can share upstream inputs, and legitimate lags or revisions can explain apparent contradictions. In sensitive areas use safe aggregate reporting; never publish personal targeting information or live operational locations.

## What every registered source should reveal

Record its publisher, geography, language, institutional type, dataset and series identifiers, original publication link, reference period, release date, revision policy, definitions, units, coverage, sampling, collection method, frequency, access method, reuse terms, cost or restriction, last verification date, and upstream dependencies. Distinguish reported observations from forecasts, estimates, imputations, and modeled series. Track methodological breaks, unavailable periods, conflicting versions, and reviewer notes.

The map should show which series are comparable, which only provide contextual clues, which repeat other sources, and where evidence is missing. Source reputation alone must not substitute for this record.

## Help make the next connection

No single institution sees the whole world. People across disciplines and communities can help one another see more of it. Choose a path through this map, bring evidence that others have overlooked, and show exactly how it changes the picture. Every connection that survives scrutiny gives the next investigation a stronger place to begin.
